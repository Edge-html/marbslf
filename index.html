How MarbsLF Works

A verified community ecosystem engineered to eliminate fake claims, protect citizen privacy, and safely return lost items and pets across Koronadal City.

Phase 1

Secure Verification & Anti-Fraud

Steps 01 to 03 • Prioritizing privacy & proof
STEP 01

1. Real Legal Identity

Citizens submit verified government ID stored encrypted in compliance with RA 10173. Public listings display only protected aliases (e.g., Verified Citizen #101) to eliminate stalking or doxxing.

Privacy-Safe Alias Protection
STEP 02

2. Real-Time Camera Proof

Found items must be photographed live inside the browser using MarbsLF camera with timestamp and location hashing. Uploading internet stock images or recycled photos is strictly blocked.

Live Timestamp Hashing
STEP 03

3. Private Ownership Q&A

To claim an item, citizens must answer confidential ownership challenge questions (e.g. serial numbers, interior contents, wallpaper, lock screen pin). Only the verified finder can inspect responses.

Zero Public Leaks
Phase 2

Safe Meetup, Handshake & Civic Reward

Steps 04 to 06 • Physical safety & fair recognition
STEP 04

4. Approved Safe Pickup Places

Returns happen strictly at registered, CCTV-monitored civic spots: Koronadal City Hall, PNP Police Stations, Zone Barangay Halls, KCC Mall, and Robinsons. Private residences and isolated alleys are forbidden.

CCTV Monitored Zones
STEP 05

5. Two-Way Return Handshake

During the in-person public exchange, the Owner taps "Item Received" and the Finder taps "Item Returned". Both digital confirmations are mandatory to resolve the listing and avoid false accusations.

Dual Mutual Confirmation
STEP 06

6. Marbs Points & Zero Scam Fee

Demanding cash ransoms or "finder fees" is unlawful and triggers instant account banning. Finders are rewarded with Marbs Points (MP) redeemable for partner merchant vouchers across Koronadal.

100% Free • Verified Rewards
