How MarbsLF Works in Koronadal City

Built to make lost-and-found easier, more trustworthy, safer, and organized than relying on scattered social media posts and physical flyers.

1. Real Legal Identity

Real name and ID verification are stored securely for safety, but public posts display only a privacy-safe alias (e.g., Verified Citizen #101).

2. Real-Time Camera Proof

Photos must be captured directly using the MarbsLF live camera with timestamp hashing to deter fake, duplicated, or internet photos.

3. Private Ownership Q&A

Claimants must answer specific identifying proof questions (serial numbers, hidden markings) reviewed privately by the finder.

4. Approved Safe Pickup Places

Returns happen ONLY at registered public spots (City Hall, Police Stations, Barangay Halls, KCC Mall, Robinsons, KNCHS Gate). No private homes allowed!

5. Two-Way Return Handshake

After meetup, Owner clicks "ITEM RECEIVED" and Finder clicks "ITEM RETURNED". Both must confirm to resolve the post and release points/rewards.

6. Marbs Points & Zero Scam Fee

Payment is NEVER required to claim or return an item. Finders receive Marbs Points (MP) redeemable for partner merchant vouchers.
